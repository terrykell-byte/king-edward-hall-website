# Glossary

Domain vocabulary for King Edward Hall (KEH).

### Entities & Facilities
- **King Edward Hall (KEH)**: The historic village hall in Lindfield, West Sussex, built in 1911 and operated as a registered charity (Charity No. 249649).
- **Newcombe Hall**: The primary main hall (15.6m x 8.6m, 200 row-seated / 100 table capacity) featuring a raised stage and PA loop.
- **Jubilee Room**: The medium-sized hall built in 1977, commonly hired for children's parties, workshops, and catering.
- **The Library**: Intimate meeting room suited for committee meetings and refreshment staging.
- **Main Kitchen / Jubilee Kitchen**: Two commercial/catering kitchens available for hire.
- **Lindfield Club**: A separate private social club operating on the first floor of the building.

### Community Events & Programs
- **Lindfield Village Day (LVD)**: Annual community summer festival on Lindfield Common featuring ~150 stalls, procession, and arena entertainment.
- **Lindfield Village Run (LVR)**: Annual cross-country 5K and 10K charity run starting from Hickmans Lane.
- **Film Nights**: Monthly community cinema showings held in the Newcombe Hall.
