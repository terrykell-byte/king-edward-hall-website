# Charity & Heritage Stewardship

## 🏛️ Charitable Trust Governance

King Edward Hall is owned and operated by the **King Edward Hall Charitable Trust**, Registered Charity No. **249649**. The hall is held in trust for the residents of Lindfield and surrounding communities in West Sussex.

## 🎱 Clear Separation: The Lindfield Club

A frequent point of public confusion on the previous website was the relationship between the Hall and the **Lindfield Club**:

| Feature | King Edward Hall (Charity) | The Lindfield Club (Private) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Ground floor halls (Newcombe, Jubilee, Library, Kitchens) | First floor |
| **Governance** | Charitable Trust (Charity No. 249649) | Private members' social & snooker club |
| **Access** | Public room hire open to anyone | Registered members and signed-in guests |
| **Bar / Snooker** | Hirers may bring alcohol/caterers | Private licensed bar and snooker tables |
| **Inquiries** | Hall Booking Secretary | Club Secretary |

The new website features an explicit, permanent FAQ and notice clarifying this distinction.

## 📜 1911 Edwardian Timeline

1. **1910**: Public meeting convened by village leaders; land opposite the duck pond secured and architect chosen.
2. **1911**: Grand opening ceremony celebrating King Edward VII's reign.
3. **1914–1918**: Hall served as a Red Cross auxiliary hospital during WWI.
4. **1968**: Formalization of modern charitable trust scheme under Charity Commission No. 249649.
5. **2011**: Centenary celebration and kitchen modernization program.
6. **2026/2027**: Digital modern platform launch preserving heritage for the next century.

## 🎁 Gift Aid Boost (+25%) Calculator

As an independent charity with no statutory local council funding, donations sustain roof repairs, heating, and conservation:
$$\text{Total to Charity} = \text{Donation} \times 1.25$$

The website includes an interactive slider where donors can see how a £50 donation automatically yields £62.50 to the Hall at no extra cost to the donor.
