## 🎱 Clear Separation: The Lindfield Club

A frequent point of public confusion on the previous website was the relationship between the Hall and **The Lindfield Club**:

| Feature | King Edward Hall (Charity) | The Lindfield Club (Private) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Ground floor halls (Newcombe, Jubilee, Library, Kitchens) | First floor |
| **Governance** | Charitable Trust (Charity No. 249649) | Independent private members' social & snooker club |
| **Access** | Public room hire open to anyone | Registered members and signed-in guests |
| **Bar / Snooker** | Premises License for functions (TEN required for alcohol sales) | Private licensed bar and snooker tables |
| **Inquiries** | Hall Booking Administrator (`roomhire@kingedwardhall.org.uk`) | Club Secretary |

The website features an explicit, permanent FAQ card clarifying this distinction.

## 📜 1911 Edwardian Timeline

1. **11th January 1911**: Designed by Walter Tower and built by Norman & Burt on land donated by Mr. Sturdy of Paxhill; opened by Col. Dudley Sampson in memory of King Edward VII.
2. **1914–1918 (WWI)**: Directed by Commandant Mrs. Godfrey Hooper as a Red Cross Convalescent Hospital for wounded Belgian and British soldiers.
3. **1939–1945 (WWII)**: Evacuee reception centre for London children and venue for social dances with Canadian soldiers stationed in Lindfield.
4. **1977**: Silver Jubilee Wing (Jubilee Room) opened to mark Queen Elizabeth II's 25th jubilee year.
5. **2014–Present**: Main Hall dedicated as **The Newcombe Hall** in memory of Brian Newcombe (55+ years of voluntary committee service).

## 🎁 Gift Aid Boost (+25%) Calculator

As an independent charity with no statutory local council funding, donations sustain roof repairs, heating upgrades, and conservation:
$$\text{Total to Charity} = \text{Donation} \times 1.25$$

Visitors can select preset donation tiers (`£10`, `£25`, `£50`, `£100`), toggle the UK taxpayer HMRC Gift Aid declaration (+25% at no extra cost), and dispatch a pre-filled donation pledge email directly to `secretary@kingedwardhall.org.uk`.
