# Community Events Hub

## Overview

King Edward Hall is the physical and administrative anchor for Lindfield's premier annual traditions. Implemented in [`src/components/EventHubs.tsx`](../../src/components/EventHubs.tsx), the Events Hub brings all community gatherings into a unified, interactive portal.

---

## 🎪 Lindfield Village Day
* **Date**: First Saturday of June (**Saturday 05/06/2027**)
* **Scale**: 49th Annual Celebration, attracting 10,000+ visitors.
* **Features**:
  * Grand Procession along Lindfield High Street (12:00 PM).
  * 150+ stalls on Lindfield Common, Arena displays, and Children's Dog Show (1:00 PM).
  * Live Music & Sussex Food Quarter until 6:30 PM.
* **Interactive Stallholder Registration**:
  * Captures Business/Charity Name and Stall Category (`crafts`, `food`, `charity`, `commercial`).
  * Generates a pre-filled `mailto:villageday@kingedwardhall.org.uk` inquiry to dispatch directly to the Village Day committee.

---

## 🏃 Lindfield Village Run (5K & 10K)
* **Date**: Late May Bank Holiday Weekend (**Sunday 23/05/2027**)
* **Courses**:
  * **5K Cross-Country** (10:45 AM start, £10.00 entry)
  * **10K Cross-Country** (10:30 AM start, £20.00 entry)
* **Registration & Race Guide**:
  * Live days-remaining countdown banner.
  * Runner pre-registration form capturing runner name, email, and distance (`5K` or `10K`), with direct `mailto:run@kingedwardhall.org.uk` confirmation link.
  * Interactive FAQ accordion covering Hickmans Lane start/finish, trail terrain, headphone safety ban, and water stations.

---

## 🎬 Film Nights Cinema
* **Venue**: Newcombe Hall
* **Ticket Price**: £9.00 per person
* **Features**:
  * Interactive screening cards (*Sense and Sensibility*, *Pressure*) that can be clicked to select the active film.
  * Online door-collection ticket reservation counter (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, or `6` tickets) with automatic total calculation and `mailto:secretary@kingedwardhall.org.uk` reservation link.

---

## 🤝 Community Sponsors Archive
* Dedicated recognition of local High Street and regional sponsors supporting Lindfield Village Day and the Village Run, paired with a direct sponsorship inquiry CTA (`villageday@kingedwardhall.org.uk`).
