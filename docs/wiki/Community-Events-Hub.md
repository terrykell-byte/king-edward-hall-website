# Community Events Hub

## Overview

King Edward Hall is the physical and administrative anchor for Lindfield's premier annual traditions. The Events Hub brings all community gatherings into a unified, interactive portal.

---

## 🎪 Lindfield Village Day
* **Date**: First Saturday of June (**Saturday 05/06/2027**)
* **Scale**: 49th Annual Celebration, attracting 10,000+ visitors.
* **Features**:
  * Grand Procession along Lindfield High Street.
  * Over 100 stalls on the Common.
  * Arena displays, live music, and children's entertainment.
* **Interactive Feature**: Online Stallholder Expression of Interest form categorized by Commercial, Charity/Community, Crafts, and Food & Drink.

---

## 🏃 Lindfield Village Run (5K & 10K)
* **Date**: Late May Bank Holiday Weekend (**Sunday 23/05/2027**)
* **Courses**:
  * **5K Fun Run**: Scenic trail suitable for all runners and families.
  * **10K Challenge**: Mixed road and scenic country trails around Lindfield parish.
* **Registration Engine**:
  * Real-time entry form capturing runner name, email, and race distance.
  * Instant confirmation view with race briefing pack delivery notification.
  * Live days-remaining countdown clock.

---

## 🎬 Film Nights Cinema
* **Venue**: Newcombe Hall
* **Ticket Price**: £9.00 per person
* **Features**:
  * Monthly cinematic screenings on a large projection screen.
  * Licensed interval bar with wine, local Sussex ales, and cinema snacks.
  * Direct seat reservation counter.
